There is only ONE panel. After connecting you always land on the instrument panel; when charging current is detected (or you tap the moon icon in its header) the same panel morphs into the dimmed night view below — no navigation, and the session recorder runs in both states. When the charge finishes, the morning report appears as a card on the panel.

Suggested additions

All confirmed readable from the DID maps we extracted — none require extra hardware. Worth deciding now which make the main screen vs. a second tab.

Charge / discharge current limits $347B/C
Shows live BMS derating in heat or cold — explains "why is my car slow / charging slowly today".
12 V battery voltage $3492
The #1 EV breakdown cause. One glance says if the aux battery is dying.
HV insulation resistance $3016*
Key safety number; a falling trend predicts isolation faults before a warning light.
Busbar + / − voltages $3481/82
Contactor and connection health — a growing gap vs. pack voltage means resistance building up.
Coolant in / out temp $3410/11
Thermal system effectiveness; pair with compressor power from the BCS ECU.
ΔV history sparkline derived
Imbalance trend across a drive/charge tells more than a single snapshot.
Session energy in / out + Wh/km derived + $A001
Integrate V×I over the session; odometer from the VECU gives true efficiency.
Charging view derived
When current is negative: charge power, charge type (AC/DC) and time-to-full estimate.
Fault rank + DTC readout $3013 + UDS 19
BMS's own severity rank plus a read-codes button — turns the app into a diagnostic tool.
HVIL & relay states $3004–3008*
Pre-charge / main relay status — instant answer to "car won't go READY".

* DID present in the TacoGotion $30xx family — needs a probe on the Punch's $34xx BMS to confirm the equivalent.
